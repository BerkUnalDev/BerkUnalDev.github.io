This policy covers all mobile games published by Berk Unal (each, "the App"), including One Tap Arcade. It explains what data the Apps handle.

Data We Collect

The Apps themselves do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. High scores and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and process data as described in Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA, where you have given permission)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data

On iOS, the App asks for tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, ads will not use your identifier for personalization. In the European Economic Area and the UK, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before any personalized ads; you may decline and will then receive non-personalized ads.

You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights (EEA/UK)

Under the GDPR you may withdraw ad-personalization consent at any time (iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking, and the in-app consent form where available). Data collected by Google is controlled by Google LLC; see their policy for exercising rights against Google.

We may update this policy; the current version will always be available at this URL.
