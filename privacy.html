Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Developer: Berk Unal (individual)
Contact: berkun_5@hotmail.com

This policy covers all mobile games published by Berk Unal (each, "the App"). It explains what data the Apps handle.

Data We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email or account, and we do not sell data. Progress, high scores and settings are stored only on your device.

Game Analytics

Some Apps use Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) to understand how the game is played so we can improve it — for example which levels are completed or failed, scores, session counts, and whether an optional rewarded ad was watched. Firebase processes:

  • Gameplay and in-app interaction events
  • A random app-instance identifier (not your name or Apple ID)
  • Device model, OS version, app version, language and approximate country (derived from IP address)

This data is used only for game analytics, is not used to identify you, and is retained according to Google Analytics retention settings. See Firebase's privacy information. Where consent is required (EEA/UK), advertising-related storage follows the choice you make in the consent form.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and process data as described in Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA, where you have given permission)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data

On iOS, the App asks for tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, ads will not use your identifier for personalization and it is not shared with Adjust. In the European Economic Area and the UK, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before any personalized ads; you may decline and will then receive non-personalized ads.

You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Install Attribution

Some Apps use Adjust (Adjust GmbH, Berlin, Germany), a mobile measurement service, to learn which of our advertising campaigns bring players to the game and how those campaigns perform. Adjust processes this data on our behalf:

  • Device identifiers: the IDFV (an identifier Apple gives each developer), an Adjust device ID, and the IDFA only if you have allowed tracking
  • IP address (used to derive approximate country and to detect fraud), device model, OS version, app version and language
  • Install and session events, the revenue from ads shown in the App, and which ad network or campaign, if any, led to the install

Adjust may share campaign results with the ad networks we advertise on, so they can measure our campaigns. In the EEA and the UK, the choices you make in the consent form are passed on with this data. See Adjust's privacy policy; you can opt your device out of Adjust measurement at adjust.com/forget-device.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights (EEA/UK)

Under the GDPR you may withdraw ad-personalization consent at any time (iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking, and the in-app Privacy settings entry in the pause menu where available). You may also contact us to ask about or delete analytics data associated with your app instance. Data collected by Google is controlled by Google LLC; see their policy for exercising rights against Google. To stop Adjust measurement for your device, use Adjust's forget-device page linked above.
