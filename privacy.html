Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Berk Unal (individual)
Contact: berkun_5@hotmail.com

This policy covers all mobile games published by Berk Unal (each, "the App"). It explains what data the Apps handle.

Data We Collect

We do not ask for your real name, email or an account, and we do not sell data. Progress, high scores and settings are stored only on your device.

Game Analytics

Some Apps use Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) to understand how the game is played so we can improve it — for example which levels are completed or failed, scores, session counts, and whether an optional rewarded ad was watched. Firebase processes:

  • Gameplay and in-app interaction events
  • A random app-instance identifier (not your name or Apple ID)
  • Device model, OS version, app version, language and approximate country (derived from IP address)

This data is used only for game analytics, is not used to identify you, and is retained according to Google Analytics retention settings. See Firebase's privacy information. Where consent is required (EEA/UK), advertising-related storage follows the choice you make in the consent form.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and process data as described in Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (on iOS the IDFA, where you have given permission; on Android the Android Advertising ID, unless you have deleted it or opted out)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data

Through AdMob mediation, some Apps (such as Rummy Card Club and Block Slide) may also show ads from these ad networks, which receive the same kinds of data listed above when they serve an ad: Unity Ads (Unity Technologies, privacy policy), InMobi (InMobi Pte. Ltd., privacy policy) and Liftoff Monetize (Liftoff Mobile, Inc., formerly Vungle, privacy policy). They follow the same tracking and consent choices described below.

On iOS, the App asks for tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, ads will not use your identifier for personalization and it is not shared with Adjust. On Android, ads and Adjust may use the Android Advertising ID; you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). In the European Economic Area and the UK, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before any personalized ads; you may decline and will then receive non-personalized ads.

You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Install Attribution

Some Apps use Adjust (Adjust GmbH, Berlin, Germany), a mobile measurement service, to learn which of our advertising campaigns bring players to the game and how those campaigns perform. Adjust processes this data on our behalf:

  • Device identifiers: on iOS the IDFV (an identifier Apple gives each developer), an Adjust device ID, and the IDFA only if you have allowed tracking; on Android the App Set ID, an Adjust device ID, and the Android Advertising ID unless you have deleted it or opted out
  • IP address (used to derive approximate country and to detect fraud), device model, OS version, app version and language
  • Install and session events, the revenue from ads shown in the App, and which ad network or campaign, if any, led to the install

Adjust may share campaign results with the ad networks we advertise on, so they can measure our campaigns. In the EEA and the UK, the choices you make in the consent form are passed on with this data. See Adjust's privacy policy; you can opt your device out of Adjust measurement at adjust.com/forget-device.

Online Play

Some Apps (Rummy Card Club) have an optional online mode where you play with other people. When you use it, the App connects to our game server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.). The server receives:

  • A random player identifier created on your device (not your name, Apple ID or advertising identifier)
  • The player name and picture you chose in the App; other players at your table see them
  • Your time-zone offset, so a daily limit on leaving matches early resets at your local midnight
  • Your moves and the preset quick-chat lines you send during a match (there is no free-text chat)

Match data is kept only while the match runs and is deleted shortly after it ends. The times you left a match early are kept for two days. If another player reports your player name, the report is kept so a name reported by several players can be replaced. Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the connection; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. All data the App sends is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Leaderboard

Rummy Card Club has a Weekly and All Time leaderboard. To build it, the App sends our game server the same random player identifier, the player name and picture you chose, and the points you earn in solo and online games. Your name, picture and points are shown to other players in the rankings. Some players in the rankings and at online tables are computer players. The App sends the identifier (and your name and picture, if you chose them) when it opens the main menu, so it can show your place. Points are kept for the leaderboard; you can ask us to delete your points and name at the contact address on this page.

Block Slide has a Weekly and All Time leaderboard of stars. When you win a level, the App sends our game server (blockslide.belinapps.com, on Cloudflare Workers) a random player identifier created on your device, the player name and picture you chose (or an automatic name such as "Player1234"), and the new stars you earned. Your name, picture and stars are shown to other players in the rankings. Some players in the rankings are computer players. Stars are kept for the leaderboard; you can ask us to delete them.

In-App Purchases

Some Apps (Block Slide) sell optional coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see or store your payment details, name or Apple ID / Google account. The App receives only a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction identifier), which it keeps on your device so a purchase is not delivered twice. Purchases are also recorded as anonymous analytics events (product and price).

Notifications

Some Apps (Block Slide) can show local reminders (for example when your lives are full or a daily gift is ready). They are scheduled on your device, no data is sent for them, and you can turn them off in the App's settings or your device settings.

Delete Your Data (Rummy Card Club)

Rummy Card Club by Berk Unal has no accounts. To delete the data linked to you:

  1. In the game, open the menu and tap your profile to see your player name.
  2. Email berkun_5@hotmail.com with the subject "Rummy Card Club data deletion" and your player name.
  3. Within 30 days we delete your player name, picture, leaderboard points and the reports linked to your random player identifier from our game server, and ask Google (Firebase Analytics) and Adjust to delete the analytics data for your app instance.

Deleted: player name, picture, leaderboard points, name reports, analytics events tied to your app instance. Kept: nothing else is stored by us; online match data is already removed after each match. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device (tickets, level, settings).

Delete Your Data (Block Slide)

Block Slide by Berk Unal has no accounts. To delete the data linked to you:

  1. In the game, tap your picture in the top bar to see your player name.
  2. Email berkun_5@hotmail.com with the subject "Block Slide data deletion" and your player name.
  3. Within 30 days we delete your player name, picture and leaderboard stars linked to your random player identifier from our game server, and ask Google (Firebase Analytics) to delete the analytics data for your app instance.

Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device (level, coins, purchases already delivered, settings). Coins bought and then deleted with the App cannot be restored.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights (EEA/UK)

Under the GDPR you may withdraw ad-personalization consent at any time (iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking; on Android, Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads; and the in-app Privacy settings entry in the pause menu where available). You may also contact us to ask about or delete analytics data associated with your app instance. Data collected by Google is controlled by Google LLC; see their policy for exercising rights against Google. To stop Adjust measurement for your device, use Adjust's forget-device page linked above.
